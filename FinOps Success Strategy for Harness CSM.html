Bridging the Gaps

A Strategic Roadmap for FinOps Excellence and Enhanced Customer Experience

The 5 Key Challenges in Our Workflow

Analysis of the current FinOps practice reveals critical friction points across the customer lifecycle, from pre-sales to ongoing support.

⚠️

Basic FinOps Support

Our talented support team lacks deep FinOps expertise, struggling to provide convincing solutions beyond simple bug fixes after customer onboarding.

⚙️

CCM Complexity

Variances in cloud provider reporting and custom enterprise deals make our "one-size-fits-all" CCM reporting inaccurate, impacting the entire toolchain.

🛠️

Pre-Sales Misconfiguration

Initial setup errors by sales engineers create significant cost issues, requiring months of corrective effort from the Professional Service team.

⏳

Prolonged Onboarding

High-impact issues arise during onboarding, and engineering delays leave customers feeling the process is incomplete, preventing basic FinOps adoption.

🔥

Reactive Engagement

We engage with major customers only after escalations, forcing us into a difficult firefighting mode to win back confidence.

Real-World Impact: The Cost of Early Misconfiguration

These case studies highlight how initial setup errors can lead to prolonged issues and significant customer frustration, underscoring the need for proactive cost calibration.

Coca-Cola: Organizational Billing Discrepancies

Initial billing configurations with Harness CCM did not account for Coca-Cola's unique organizational-level billing arrangements. This led to incorrect reporting of savings from autostopping and asset governance for 4-5 months post-onboarding, requiring extensive engineering effort to resolve. This highlights how crucial accurate initial setup is, especially for large enterprises with complex billing structures.

Coles: Kubernetes Cost Reporting Challenges

When the PS team was doing onboarding, it was found that due to several reasons, Kubernetes cost reporting was not good, and some of the issues were because the delegates were not properly sized. However, the PS team was not involved during the initial configuration, and it took months to discover this issue after long technical sessions back and forth with engineering. Till date, this is an issue with Coles.

Transunion: Cloudability Cost Mismatch

Transunion experienced an ongoing cost mismatch where customer expectation to match data with the existing Cloudability took several turnaround times. Later on, it was discovered that they had some enterprise discounts with GCP which was causing cost mismatch. This took some time to realize, as at times, due to lack of information on the customer side as well, the default tool behavior and cloud companies writing data to CUR files cause issues like these.

A 5-Point Plan for Transformation

To address these challenges head-on, we propose a strategic overhaul of our support, processes, and engineering priorities.

1. Introduce Premium Technical Support

Launch a chargeable, expert-led support tier offering proactive FinOps consulting, ensuring customers continuously derive value and have access to deep expertise.

2. Create a "Cost Calibration" Team

Dedicate a small engineering team to assist Sales Engineers, ensuring cost reporting is accurate from day one and eliminating costly downstream corrections.

3. Redefine "Onboarding Complete"

Professional Service (PS) teams must retain ownership and follow-up until all customer-blocking P1 issues are fully resolved, leveraging the rapport they've built.

4. Implement Warm Handovers to CSMs

Establish a formal, technical handover from PS to CSMs. If new technical issues arise, our expert team must be engaged immediately for technical cadence calls, not just ticket follow-ups.

5. Prioritize Enhancements by Impact

Shift engineering focus to prioritize feature enhancements based on their strategic value and potential impact across the customer base, not just the size of the requesting customer.
